# NoShortVideos

A Chrome extension designed to help you regain focus by hiding distracting short-form video content from your browser. 

In the age of algorithmic content, it's easy to fall down a rabbit hole of endless scrolling. NoShortVideos actively manipulates the DOM to hide addictive elements like YouTube Shorts, TikTok feeds, and Instagram Reels without completely blocking the useful parts of these platforms.

## Features (Planned)
- **YouTube**: Hides the Shorts shelf, sidebar links, and Shorts in search results.
- **Instagram**: Hides Reels from the feed and sidebar.
- **TikTok**: Hides the main distracting feeds.
- **Popup UI**: Easily toggle the blocking functionality on and off, with additional settings to customize your experience.
